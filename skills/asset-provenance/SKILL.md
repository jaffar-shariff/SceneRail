# Asset and Source Provenance

A URL or visible reference does not automatically grant reuse permission.

## Asset ledger fields
Record, where applicable:
- asset ID and local path;
- creator/source and URL or user-supplied origin;
- license/permission evidence;
- acquisition date and attribution requirements;
- transformations;
- intended use;
- known limitations or unresolved rights.

## Research claim ledger
For each important factual claim: claim text, source, publication date if relevant, evidence summary, confidence/limitations, and whether the evidence is primary or secondary.

## Rules
- Prefer original procedural visuals, user-owned material, public-domain work, or media with a clearly compatible license.
- Do not assume search results can be reused.
- Do not transmit private audio/video to external services without explicit approval.
- Never commit API keys, `.env`, private footage, customer data, or protected source files.
- Keep source provenance distinct from aesthetic choices.
- If rights cannot be established, choose a safer alternative or clearly disclose the risk before publication.
